Google shipped **Nano Banana Pro** (Gemini 3 Pro Image) to general availability in June 2026. And it brings something AI imagery had never solved: **legible text**.

## Text you can read, not gibberish

It's the first model where asking to "add the text 'Sale' in white on the product" produces real, readable text, with 94%+ accuracy, in any language. For mockups, packaging, posters and international content, it's a before-and-after.

## 4K in seconds

It generates native 4K images (up to 4096×4096) in under 12 seconds. Campaign resolution with no wait.

## What it solves for your brand

Until now, any text on a generated image had to be fixed by hand. With this, a packshot with a claim, a poster or a price tag come out in one pass. Less retouching, more consistency.
