**Kling 3.0** (released February 2026) has earned the title of AI video's value champion. Not because it's cheap, but because of what it delivers at its price.

## Multi-shot storytelling

It generates **up to 6 shots per run**, and the AI handles transitions, angles, shot-reverse-shot and movement. Instead of an isolated clip, you get a mini-sequence with intent.

## 4K and native audio

**Native 4K** output and integrated multilingual audio. Its music-sync feature lets you upload a track and have the video move to the beat, very useful for social.

## Unbeatable cost

At ~$0.50 per clip it's the most cost-effective option for volume: teams generating 100+ clips a month save thousands versus other options. It also has the industry's most generous free tier.
