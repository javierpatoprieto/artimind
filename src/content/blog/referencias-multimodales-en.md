The biggest change of 2026 isn't just more resolution: it's **how you control** the model. Seedance 2.5 accepts up to 30 images, 10 video clips and 10 audio clips as reference in a single pass.

## From prompt to brief

You used to describe in words and cross your fingers. Now you attach style, motion, character and sound references. It's the difference between asking and directing.

## Why it matters to you

With references, your brand leads: palette, mood, rhythm and consistency across pieces get genuinely locked in. Less drift, more "this is mine."
