AI stopped being an experiment in fashion ecommerce: over 60% of brands now use at least one AI tool in content production, up from 35% in 2024. These are the five trends leading 2026.

## 1. Generative virtual try-on

Seeing the garment on yourself before buying cuts returns by 25% to 48%. Generative try-on, with no complex 3D modeling, is the dominant trend.

## 2. Campaigns generated at scale

Instead of a shoot per collection, brands generate models, scenes and campaign visuals in minutes, starting from product photos. Up to 90% lower photography cost.

## 3. Personalized fit

Size personalization and full-outfit visualization are the fastest-growing capabilities. The customer sees *their* combination, not a generic one.

## 4. Commerce inside content

Instagram and TikTok are piloting AR try-on inside content itself: discover and try in the same session.
