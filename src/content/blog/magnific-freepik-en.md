In April 2026, Freepik reorganized around the **Magnific** name, combining its large stock library with a full suite of AI image, video, audio and 3D tools alongside the upscaler. As an official partner, here's what matters.

## Upscaling up to 16x

Magnific increases resolution **up to 16x** while adding realistic texture, light and detail. It doesn't just enlarge: it reconstructs. That's what takes an image from "good" to "campaign."

## Relight: change the lighting

The **Relight** feature lets you completely change a scene's lighting, including for product and portrait. Adjusting light without reshooting is gold for catalog consistency.

## Precision vs Creative

**Precision** mode for fidelity (photography) and **Creative** mode to push the result further. Sharper, faster and more controlled than ever.
