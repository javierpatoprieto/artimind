OpenAI confirmed a two-stage shutdown of **Sora 2**: the consumer app closed on April 26, 2026 and the API sunsets on September 24, 2026. If it was in your workflow, it's time to move.

## Why it matters

Sora 2 delivered some of the most convincing physical motion. Its retirement leaves a gap, but also a lesson: don't tie your brand to a single model.

## Where to migrate

The field is well covered. For realism and audio, **Veo 3.1**. For story and cost, **Kling 3.0**. For length and 4K, **Seedance 2.5**. The choice depends on the piece.
