With a dozen frontier models, the question is no longer "which is best?" but "which for this?". Here's how I decide on each project.

## Start with the goal

Dialogue and realism? Veo 3.1. Multi-shot story and cost? Kling 3.0. Length and 4K? Seedance 2.5. The goal beats the hype of the month.

## Budget and volume

For 100+ clips a month, cost per clip decides. Kling wins on volume; Veo is reserved for hero pieces. Mixing models by each piece's role is the efficient move.

## Timeline

If the deadline is tight, the model that iterates fast and nails the final format first wins. Speed is a model decision too.
