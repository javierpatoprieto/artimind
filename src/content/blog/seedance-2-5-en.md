ByteDance unveiled **Seedance 2.5** at its Volcano Engine FORCE conference on June 23, 2026, with a full release planned for July 31. It's a big step for anyone producing video with AI. Here's what matters.

## 30 seconds in a single take

Seedance 2.5 doubles 2.0's length: from 15 to **30 seconds generated in one pass**. And it's not stretching a single moment: within those 30 seconds the model organizes connected shots with setup, development, turning point and resolution. It starts to feel like a piece, not a loose clip.

## Native 4K

It generates **native 4K** with sharp textures, natural faces and cinematic depth. For campaign work this matters: it's the difference between an "AI clip" and one that holds up on a big screen.

## Multimodal referencing

In a single pass it accepts up to **30 images, 10 video clips and 10 audio clips** as reference, plus render, motion and creative styles. That's real directing, not praying to a prompt.
