A year ago there were two or three serious names in AI video. Today there are a dozen frontier models and native synced audio is table stakes. Here's the map at mid-2026.

## The leaders

On independent rankings, ByteDance's **Seedance 2.0** and Alibaba's models fight for the top. Seedance stands out in audio generation and now, with 2.5, in length and 4K.

## Veo 3.1: broadcast quality

Google DeepMind's **Veo 3.1** targets broadcast quality: realistic physics, 48 kHz synced audio and strong prompt adherence. Its edge is lip-sync and film-like texture.

## Kling 3.0: value and story

**Kling 3.0** is the value champion (~$0.50/clip) and shines at multi-shot storytelling, up to 6 shots per run with automatic transitions. It also has the most generous free tier.

## Sora 2 bows out

**Sora 2** still delivers the most physically convincing motion, but OpenAI confirmed a two-stage shutdown: the app closed in April and the API sunsets on September 24, 2026.
