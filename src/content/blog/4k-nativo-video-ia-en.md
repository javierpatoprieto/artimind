For a while, AI video smelled like "AI": just-enough resolution, soft edges. In 2026, **native 4K** is in the frontier models, and that changes where you can use it.

## Native, not upscaled

4K generated at source isn't the same as enlarging a 1080p. There's real texture, smooth gradients and detail that survives the zoom. Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0 and Veo already deliver it.

## Why it matters for campaigns

Billboards, cinema, TV, in-store screens: places where a soft clip gives the AI away. In 4K, the piece competes with traditional production.
